About Me

My Background

I'm a graduate student in Technical Communication with an interest in the ways human-centered design shapes our interactions with technology, information, and the world around us.

Through my academic work, I explore how technical communication can improve learning experiences and make scientific and health-related information more accessible to diverse audiences. I hope to translate my research and learning into meaningful work as a technical communicator. My time in academia has strengthened my skills in research, writing, design, project management, and collaboration, and I look forward to applying these skills in future professional opportunities.

Outside of my academic work, I enjoy reading a good novel, practicing my culinary skills, exploring the outdoors, and spending time with family and friends.

Research Interests

User Experience Design

How users interact with digital products, navigate information, and adopt new technologies. I am particularly interested in usability, accessibility, and user-centered research.

Instructional Design

How communication and design can support learning. My interests include educational technologies, instructional materials, and audience-centered learning experiences.

Science Communication

How scientific knowledge and complex information can be communicated effectively to diverse audiences through accessible language and meaningful design.

Rhetoric of Health and Medicine

How language, technology, and communication influence healthcare experiences, medical information, and relationships between professionals and patients.

MY APPROACH

Communication should begin with people.

Effective communication starts with understanding the people who will use, interpret, and engage with information. Whether developing instructional materials, conducting user research, or analyzing communication practices, I value audience awareness, accessibility, rhetorical decision-making, and the social contexts that shape communication.
